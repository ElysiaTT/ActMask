\section{Conclusion}
\bench treats dynamic verification as an evaluation-hygiene problem. By progressively matching static, unordered, recent-history, and candidate-degeneracy channels while retaining execution-grounded outcomes, it makes a bounded claim about temporal evidence use. The frozen state-only result and audits support a benchmark contribution, not a claim about visual robotics or deployment. A minimal visual validation is a logical next test, but requires a separate authorized protocol.
