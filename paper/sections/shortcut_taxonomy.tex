\section{Shortcut taxonomy}
\paragraph{Static geometry and action-template shortcuts.} A score can depend on the final state or candidate index rather than a history. Static matching makes paired final observations and candidate actions equal; static/action controls then diagnose residual use of such fields.
\paragraph{Order-invariant motion shortcut.} Aggregate displacement or an unordered multiset can encode the answer even when current geometry is matched. Signed-order pairs hold unordered information fixed and reverse temporal direction; unordered and sorted-history controls diagnose this route.
\paragraph{Final-two-frame shortcut.} A finite-difference estimator can succeed from a recent suffix. The nonlinear protocol gives paired histories common final two frames and evaluates a final-two-only control.
\paragraph{Invalid intervention and ranking degeneracy.} A generic full reversal is not automatically a counterfactual partner: it can move common synchronization frames and invalidate timestamp alignment. Likewise, many candidates with one common outcome make ranking non-causal. We therefore use the exact early-frame swap and audit per-world outcome diversity.
