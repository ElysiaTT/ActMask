\section{Limitations}
This is a state-observation benchmark, not a visual one. It has no RGB-D noise, tracking, correspondence ambiguity, language conditioning, real-robot validation, or end-to-end VLA integration. Its task families are procedural and GPU-PhysX based, with a controlled TCP proxy rather than a full manipulation policy. Benchmark construction necessarily uses knowledge of the intended counterfactual factors, and limited embodiment diversity leaves simulator-specific structure possible. Perfect scores on deliberately controlled pairs should be read as diagnostic separation, not as general physical reasoning. The GRU is lightweight and is not claimed to be a general dynamics reasoner, a world model, or superior to robot policies outside these families.
