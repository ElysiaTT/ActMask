\section{Baselines}
We distinguish controls, fair analytic estimators, and learned diagnostics. Static MLP, action-only, candidate-template-only, and unordered-history models test prohibited shortcut routes. LastTwo finite difference tests the final-two suffix. MultiFrameLinearVelocity, RobustLinearVelocity, constant-acceleration fitting, and Kalman-style estimators are analytic baselines restricted to the fair observable contract. Validation selects the final fair analytic comparator; hidden-state or oracle methods are excluded from that comparison. GRU is the selected lightweight temporal learned model; TemporalConv1D and other learned models are diagnostic controls rather than evidence of a model sweep. Historical methods that failed a previous protocol remain historical diagnostics.
