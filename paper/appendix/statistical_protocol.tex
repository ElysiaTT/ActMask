\section{Statistical protocol}
Models and analytic comparators are selected using validation records. The final GRU-versus-analytic effect uses grouped bootstrap over 6,000 frozen groups, yielding +0.400 and [0.3951, 0.4052]. The interval therefore expresses variation over the stated grouping unit, not a claim of 6,000 independent physical systems. OOD ranges are disjoint: damping/drive ranges move from [0.12,0.18]/[1.15,1.45] to [0.26,0.32]/[1.85,2.15], and delay from [0,1] to [2,3]; held mechanism changes the response law.
