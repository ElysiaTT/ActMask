\section{Additional historical results}
The sequence 3P, 3Q, 3R, and v1 is a design audit. In 3P, original static performance is high while matched static pair accuracy is 0.5. In 3Q, static/action/unordered/sorted controls are 0.5 and signed finite difference/ordered temporal MLP are 1.0 in four tasks. The robust 3R stage required a human decision because its nonlinear OOD gate was not met. V1 explicitly cannot support a nonlinear-OOD-pass claim because generic reversal was invalid and C20 candidates were degenerate. These observations motivate v2; they are not substituted for final v2 evidence.
